Step 1 – per program


Problem


-Researchers waste time and money chasing cross-disease hypotheses that look promising but fail late.


-Literature volume hides weak or contradictory signals.


-Failure happens after months or years instead of being killed early.




Solution


-Automatically triage and downgrade weak ideas early.


-Surface why something is risky before experiments start.


-Provide a fast “do not proceed yet” filter.




How it is different


-Most tools surface positive signals only.


-This system explicitly models negative evidence and conflict.


-It is a kill-first engine, not a discovery hype engine.





Step 2 – per program


Demand


High in:


-Academic labs with limited budgets.


-Pharma early discovery teams.


-Translational medicine units.




-Especially valuable where early kill saves millions.




Ask inside medicine


-“Is this idea worth touching at all?”


-“Where will this fail if we try?”


-“What evidence must exist before we even design an experiment?”
