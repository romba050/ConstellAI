2. CAFs-VEGF Knockdown Experiment (~€22,000, 8 weeks) — Use CRISPR-Cas9 to knock down VEGF expression in CAFs and examine the effect on tumor growth in vivo. | kill if: if tumor growth is not significantly reduced by VEGF knockdown, hypothesis is rejected.

CAFs-secretome Analysis Experiment (~€15,000, 4 weeks) — Compare the secretome of CAFs from pancreatic ductal adenocarcinoma patients with and without high levels of VEGF to identify other potential angiogenic factors. | kill if: if no significant difference is found in the secretome, hypothesis is rejected.
4. VEGF-Blocking Antibody Experiment (~€20,000, 6 weeks) — Treat mice with pancreatic ductal adenocarcinoma with VEGF-blocking antibody and examine the effect on tumor growth and angiogenesis. | kill if: if treatment with VEGF-blocking antibody does not significantly reduce tumor growth, hypothesis is rejected.
5. CAFs-Co-culture Experiment (~€12,000, 4 weeks) — Co-cultivate CAFs with pancreatic ductal adenocarcinoma cells in vitro and examine the effect on cell proliferation and angiogenesis. | kill if: if co-culture does not significantly enhance cell proliferation or angiogenesis, hypothesis is rejected.
