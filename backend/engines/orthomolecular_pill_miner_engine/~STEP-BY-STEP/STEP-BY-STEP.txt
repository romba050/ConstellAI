Step 1 – per program

Problem

Researchers intuitively guess “natural mimics” of drugs without structured mapping.

This leads to:

hand-wavy supplement logic,

mechanistic overclaims,

and unsafe substitution thinking.

Solution

A drug-anchored mechanistic decomposition engine:

start from a known drug,

extract its signaling footprint,

identify non-drug compounds that hit overlapping downstream programs.

Explicitly frames results as mechanistic comparison, not treatment.

How it is different from what’s available

Most tools either:

list targets, or

list supplements.

This tool:

forces a drug-first anchor,

reasons at pathway convergence level,

and produces a structured memo, not advice.

Step 2 – per program

Demand

Medium–high in:

mechanism-centric oncology research,

nutraceutical R&D (preclinical only),

academic hypothesis framing.

Low for direct clinical use (by design).

Ask inside medicine

“What parts of a drug’s effect are pathway-driven vs molecule-specific?”

“Are there non-drug compounds that stress the same signaling programs?”

“Which mechanisms are shared, and which are unique to the drug?”

“Where does mechanistic similarity clearly stop?”
