# Challenge criteria and the implemented milestone

The decision supported by this prototype is: **Which sourced therapeutic hypothesis
deserves further investigation, and what must researchers validate next?**
Maria receives a research direction, its evidence and limitations, and a proposed
validation question. This is a decision-preparation milestone toward research.

| Criterion | Implemented evidence | Practical limit |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Knowledge graph quality | Original 6,457-disease atlas plus a reviewed therapeutic graph linking 12 entity types. Each edge exposes source, relationship, observed/inferred status, confidence basis, scope and contrary findings. | Graph structure and citation closure are tested; completeness and biological truth require expert review. |
| Evidence integrity | Native Literature reviews tab checked, primary papers curated, studies dated, formulations and variant contexts distinguished, source-to-assessment traceability in the app and documentation. | Review is focused on STXBP1/CPLX1; source existence and quoted support do not establish clinical efficacy. |
| Patient progress | Search → mechanism → graph → auditable candidate comparison → proposed experiment and resources. CPLX1 shows known biology alongside a drug-evidence gap. | No patient treatment decision, trial eligibility determination, actual experiment or collaboration is claimed. |
| 10× acceleration thesis | The app assembles disconnected identity, mechanism, paper/study evidence, safety comparison and validation planning in one cached workflow. | No exact time reduction has been measured. Expert review, experiments, ethics, funding and partner replies retain their real duration. |
| Product craft / ambition | Dark, responsive Maria journey; short candidate facets; expandable technical graph, literature review and arithmetic; explicit insufficient evidence; one repeatable cached demo. | Broad atlas navigation has deeper coverage than therapeutic curation. Live AI and public hosting need external activation/verification. |

## Measure the acceleration honestly

Use a fresh participant and the same disease question for a manual-source condition
and a ConstellAI condition, counterbalancing order to reduce learning effects.
Record time to identify the gene/mechanism, find defensible candidate evidence,
recognise important limitations, and produce a source-backed next experiment and
resource list. Have an expert score citation correctness and harmful omissions.
Report time, error rate, completion and scope together. Do not substitute API
latency or a curator's familiar demo for a patient-group usability benchmark.

The reviewed tab's months-to-days figures are illustrative assumptions. Software
does not establish a 10× clinical development timeline from those assumptions.

## Submission handoff

Use the 60-second script in `THERAPEUTIC_DEMO.md`. Keep the variant abstention
and CPLX1 evidence gap in the story. The teammate can add the server key after
GitHub upload; an actual provider call and public deployment remain separate
checks. Team video recording and organizer submission require the team's actions.

`CHALLENGE_SUBMISSION.md` maps the six-page brief to concrete software coverage,
records the required artifacts and supplies a team-video speaking outline.
Both interfaces now focus on Maria, as requested; the other brief roles are
stakeholders served by her research collaboration rather than UI persona modes.
