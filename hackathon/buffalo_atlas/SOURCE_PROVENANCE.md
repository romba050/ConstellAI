## Scope and curation

Each graph node/edge stores source IDs plus resolved source metadata, contextual confidence, observed/inferred/proposed status and a contradiction-review field. The snapshot is curated rather than continuously synchronized. Retrieval dates refer to implementation-time source lookup, not a patient observation. PubMed identifiers resolve to indexed papers; the local summaries paraphrase source evidence and are not full-text republication. The one exact AI extraction span is displayed for review.

Angelman: maternal neuronal UBE3A deficiency; primary unsilencing, neuronal-model and transcriptional-spillover studies. Topotecan evidence is preclinical/cellular. (S)-PHA533533 is a research compound; CDK2/CDK5 target biology is recorded as off-signature, and its unsilencing is not attributed to those targets. The published UBE3A-ATS ASO comparator is not equated to ION582.

Shared community/model path: Chamberlain et al. PMID 20876107, paired Angelman/PWS iPSC models. The demonstrated commonality is model/imprinting context, not clinical drug response. PWS is complex and often multigene. UBE3A-only maternal duplication case PMID 25884337 supplies a dosage counterexample; its exclusion from the restoration cluster is an inference. Do not treat all Dup15q as UBE3A-only disease.

Known limiting evidence: Meng et al. report incomplete phenotype rescue despite restored expression. The graph does not pretend to have reviewed all contradictions. Developmental timing, deletion context, functional rescue, delivery and tolerability remain gaps.

## Discovery and organization limits

The pre-existing GARD-derived catalog has 6,131 name records, includes non-monogenic conditions and is not independently revalidated end-to-end. Outside the curated pilots the app returns discovery only, a linked authoritative resource and no therapeutic graph/rankings. Official patient organizations are navigation resources, not partners. Buffalo resources are linked generically; no pilot-specific Buffalo affiliation is asserted. Publication teams, model procurement, licensing and current availability are unverified.

## Extending the evidence set

Add source metadata first; curate context-specific mechanism/variant direction and exact relation claims; distinguish inference/proposal from observation; review contradictions; validate all graph links; run regression tests. A legitimate citation alone does not prove that the source entails a specific claim. An OpenAI exact-span check also cannot replace scientific semantic review.

## Direct-pair prior records

Three preclinical mechanism observations (topotecan, published UBE3A-ATS antisense perturbation, (S)-PHA533533 for Angelman) and one clinical symptom RCT reference (ganaxolone/CDKL5) are stored separately as `known_relationships`. These are not four clinical disease-modifying repurposing successes. The prior never transfers to PWS or an unrelated disease, and clinical symptom evidence does not fabricate a causal gene-restoration effect.
