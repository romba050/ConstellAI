## Literature and evidence-quality metadata

All 11 PMID identities and bibliographic fields were checked with the official NCBI PubMed EFetch API. The snapshot stores PMID, DOI, PMCID/PMC link when indexed, full title, authors, journal, PubMed-indexed year, publication types, extracted/paraphrased claim, claim kind, study design, sample context, retrieval URL/date and linked correction notices. DOI or denominator absence is explicit. Indexed online/issue years are not silently assumed identical.

No journal allowlist is used. BMJ, Journal of Internal Medicine, Cureus and any other relevant PubMed-indexed journal can contribute evidence. Journal prestige and author identity never alter MEDR5 priority. Citation identity alone does not prove entailment or correctness. No Ovid dependency or licensed institutional login is introduced.

Assay examples are deliberately labelled as examples, not a single study-wide N. Cells, cultures, coverslips, technical quadruplicates, donors and randomised participants are different units. The ganaxolone paper reports 101 randomised participants and a 100-person primary-endpoint analysis; that symptom endpoint does not establish causal CDKL5 restoration. PubMed links its erratum PMID:35716695; correction details have not been independently reviewed. Contradiction review remains incomplete.

## Core biology: identifiers, variants and mapping scope

- MONDO:0007113 / Angelman; MONDO:0100039 / broader CDKL5 disorder; MONDO:0012812 / the narrower STXBP1 DEE4 condition. The complete CACNA1A umbrella is not falsely equated to one narrower ontology disease. Synonyms and cross-reference context are retained in source metadata.
- HPO supplies terminology for ataxia and seizure features in the graph. Disease-specific sources support presence; an HPO definition alone does not demonstrate a disease association. Other verified HPO terms remain in the source registry without forced phenotype mappings. Generic feature overlap is not a therapy-transfer argument.
- ClinVar VCV000155984.20 / NM_130839.5(UBE3A):c.2567_2568del (p.Lys856fs) is a public illustrative record. Broader aggregation is Pathogenic/Likely pathogenic; Angelman-specific RCV000144307.20 is Pathogenic with criteria provided, multiple submitters and no conflicts, evaluated 2025-07-30. It is not expert-panel review. The graph does not convert pathogenicity into a functional direction, patient diagnosis, intervention effect or safety claim. Transcript and parent-of-origin context require review.
- OMIM:105830 and ORPHA:72 are verified Angelman cross-references in MONDO. Direct OMIM retrieval returned HTTP 403; direct Orphanet retrieval returned a browser-check page. No restricted text, registry, expert-centre inventory or access claim was imported. Their records explicitly support identifiers only.

## Reproduction and refresh

Run `tools/reproduce_dataset.py` from the README command to reproduce the versioned curated assertions, source registry, four graphs, deterministic reasoning, benchmark and output hash manifest offline. Human-curated evidence and scope decisions are the inputs; this is not autonomous discovery or a systematic literature review. The optional PubMed metadata refresh writes a separate bibliography-only proposal and never promotes claims or changes scores. Full copyrighted abstracts/text and patient-level records are not redistributed. `data/source_review_receipts.json` records hashes of transient reviewer inputs; it does not pretend those local files are permanent public archives.

The inherited 6,131-entry GARD catalog is discovery-only, includes non-monogenic conditions, and is not 6,131 validated treatment mechanisms. Outside curated pilots there is no fabricated therapeutic ranking.

## Known limits

Expression restoration is not whole-disease rescue. The Meng study reports incomplete phenotype rescue. Human cell findings are not clinical trials. Developmental timing, deletion context, functional rescue, exposure, delivery and long-term tolerability remain unresolved. Clinical safety is shown separately from mechanistic fit; spillover counts and combinations do not certify safety. Numeric uncertainty policy weights are uncalibrated and do not use journal prestige.
